Meet Your Agent

Sanjeev Kataria

Realtor® · Alta Realty Group CA, Inc. · DRE #02044107

Sanjeev Kataria has spent over a decade helping Southern California families find their next home and helping sellers maximize the value of theirs. He holds a Master's degree in Computer Science, bringing a rare blend of analytical precision and market insight to every transaction — from pricing strategy and negotiation to paperwork and closing logistics.

Based in Newport Beach and fluent in English and Hindi, Sanjeev works with first-time buyers, move-up families, investors, and sellers throughout Orange County and Riverside County. His clients consistently point to his responsiveness, patience, and honest, data-driven advice as the reason they refer him to friends and family.
